    Basics

    Full charge is recommended before first use. There are two ways to charge: Basic charging — any USB power source. Tracked charging — requires a computer USB port, which allows charge time logging.

    1. 1Switch power ON before plugging in. The Power Indicator (yellow LED) lights up, and the start-up sequence of LEDs bounce across the top.
    2. 2Plug in the USB-C power cord. The Charge Indicator (red LED) begins blinking. If a computer port is detected, the pink/blue LEDs pulse (after 30 seconds) to confirm Tracked charging is active.

    Sleep (when not in Tracked charging): after 5 minutes of inactivity, pink/blue LEDs indicate sleep mode. Use Touchpad to wake at any time. See ? for more info.

    I/O Ports

    3.5 mm jacks:

    • Line In — direct analog line pass-through to Tracker Mini
    • Line Out — direct analog line pass-through from Tracker Mini
    • MIDI Out — direct digital line pass-through from Tracker Mini

    Speakers & Amp

    Independent analog amplifier

    • Separate from the digital MIDI system
    • The switch controls it independently; the green indicator shows when it is on
    • Switch it off when not in use: while on, it draws directly from the battery—even when the CPU is asleep

    Controls

    Levers and touchpad can map to any MIDI CC in the Configurator app.
    Octave buttons are fixed.

    • Levers: left, right, and push are three independent controls
    • Touchpad: continuous analog input for real-time value changes

    Tracked Charging

    1. 1 Connect USB-C to a computer port. After 30 seconds without key, lever, or touch input, the pink/blue LEDs pulse to confirm tracked charging is active. Any input hides the pulse and restarts the 30-second timer.
    2. 2 First-time calibration takes about 5 hours of charge time total. Sessions don't need to be back-to-back — progress is saved and picks up automatically next time you charge this way.
    3. 3Once calibrated, tracking continues automatically on every charge after that — no need to repeat it.
    4. 4Enable Battery Monitoring in the Configurator App to see your percentage — it's hidden by default. You can also override the value manually there if needed.

    Bluetooth

    Bluetooth is off by default to maximize battery life.
    To enable Bluetooth, follow these steps:

    1. 1 Squeeze both levers toward each other and hold.
    2. 2All LEDs light up, then pink/blue LEDs blink with increasing speed.
    3. 3 Release when all LEDs turn off — Bluetooth enabled.
    Use the same gesture to disable Bluetooth.
    Bluetooth is automatically disabled when entering sleep mode.

    Configurator App

    Once Bluetooth is enabled, follow these steps in the KB1 Configurator web app:

    1. 1Tap the Bluetooth icon → select your device from the pairing dialog.
    2. 2Load presets, explore settings, try new combos, then, tap the amber arrow to upload. Repeat.
    Designed for mobile:
    Android — Chrome, Edge, Opera.
    iOS — V Browser recommended

    Tracker Setup

    Tracker Settings

    On the Tracker Mini open Menu → Config → MIDI

    Set:

    1. 1Notes in to MIDI In jack
    2. 2Notes in channel to CH1
    3. 3CC in to MIDI In jack
    4. 4CC in channel to CH1
    5. 5Middle C to C-4

    About Flash Tools

    KB1 Flash Tool is free and open source. The firmware, config app, and flash tool are all MIT licensed and available on GitHub.

    →GitHub Repositories →Pocket MIDI Website
