    Basic Charging

    FIRST. Power it on.
    That's right, turn it on first.
    1. 1 When switched ON, the Power Indicator (yellow LED) will light up, and the start-up sequence of LEDs will bounce across the top.
    2. 2Plug in the USB-C power cord. The Charge Indicator (red LED) should begin blinking. (If it doesn't blink, unplug USB, wait 5 seconds, reconnect.)

    Smart Charging

    For charging progress to be detected, saved, and accumulated, a Computer Port is required.

    1. 1 When switched ON, the Power Indicator (yellow LED) will light up, and the start-up sequence of LEDs will bounce across the top.
    2. 2Connect USB-C to a computer port. After 30 seconds (if no keys are pressed), the pink/blue LEDs will begin pulsing to confirm tracked charging is active, and will stop when the estimated charge time is complete, or when key press is detected.

    Bluetooth

    Bluetooth is off by default to maximize battery life.
    To enable Bluetooth, follow these steps:

    1. 1Squeeze both levers toward each other and hold 3 seconds.
    2. 2Octave LEDs light, then pink/blue LEDs pulse with increasing speed.
    3. 3 Release when all LEDs turn off — Bluetooth enabled.
    Use the same gesture to disable Bluetooth.
    Bluetooth is automatically disabled when entering sleep mode.

    Configurator

    Once Bluetooth is enabled, follow these steps in the KB1 Configurator web app:

    1. 1Tap the Bluetooth icon → select your device from the pairing dialog.
    2. 2Load presets, explore settings, try new combos, then, tap the amber arrow to upload. Repeat.
    Designed for mobile:
     Android — Chrome, Edge, Opera.
    iOS — V Browser (Safari has no Web Bluetooth).

    Tracker Setup

    Tracker Settings

    On the Tracker Mini open Menu → Config → MIDI

    Set:

    1. 1Notes in to MIDI In jack
    2. 2Notes in channel to CH1
    3. 3CC in to MIDI In jack
    4. 4CC in channel to CH1
    5. 5Middle C to C-4
